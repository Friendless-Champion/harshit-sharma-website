On FRC Team 2220 Blue Twilight, I lead student CAD work. A design has to fit, move, and survive testing before it becomes part of a competition robot; that process is what makes engineering tangible to me.

I also want to understand who new technology affects and how decisions about it get made. Debate and policy research help me ask those questions; advocacy and STEM outreach let me discuss them with people outside the classroom.

My postsecondary coursework spans engineering, policy, psychology, writing, and statistics across four institutions. Outside class and the robotics shop, I study languages, take part in Model UN and Science Olympiad, and enjoy performing arts.
