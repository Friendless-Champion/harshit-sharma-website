From competitor
to coach.

I chose Public Forum, a two-on-two debate event where speeches become shorter as the round progresses, with three minutes of crossfire between each set of speeches.

I competed from freshman through junior year. My junior season was my strongest: my partner and I won the Farmington Invitational with a 5–1 record and a unanimous 3–0 final. I finished with a 47.5% career win rate and earned the NSDA Degree of Special Distinction in 2025.

I later mentored three teams: one novice team and two junior varsity teams. I helped one JV team prepare specifically for the state tournament and supported the other two for about a month; the novice team finished seventh out of 80 teams.
