What the work
looked like.

Through Connexions Tutoring, I taught Hindi online for 10 hours across five weeks. We worked on basic vocabulary and everyday sentences, then began learning Hindi letters and vowels. We were still working through them when our time together ended.

I grew up knowing Hindi alongside English, but at the same time I was tutoring, I was learning Spanish and often struggled with it. My students spoke English as their first language and were learning Hindi, so I tried to put myself in their shoes. I thought about what I had struggled with and what helped me understand, then used that perspective to help them.

Learning a language can be difficult, and I wanted my students to know it was okay to struggle. I listened to where they needed help, explained ideas at their pace and gave them time to practice. Patience and care mattered as much as the words we were learning.
