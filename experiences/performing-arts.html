What the work
looked like.

I used to hum music from TV shows and movies I liked, then try to understand how to play it. I played piano from fall 2017 to fall 2023. I started French horn in summer 2019 and still play it today.

Across fourteen piano recitals and more than twenty-two band concerts, I have learned to pay attention to more than my own part. In band, someone can miss a note or lose their place. I need to listen to the people around me, adapt and keep playing.

I still hear a song I like and want to figure out its melody. The music from Interstellar is one I keep coming back to. Recognizing something by ear and trying it on an instrument is part of why I started learning music, and why I still enjoy it.

Top skills utilized

  • Active listening
  • Ensemble playing
  • Musical interpretation
  • Adaptability
